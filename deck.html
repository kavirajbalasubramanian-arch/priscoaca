The code sets

Six code sets, one connected skill.

ICD-10-CM · I10

Essential hypertension. Explains why the patient was seen.

CPT® · 99214

Office visit, established patient. Explains what was done.

ICD-10-PCS · 0DTJ4ZZ

Inpatient procedure. Seven characters, each with a meaning.

HCPCS II · A0428

Ambulance, basic life support. Covers what CPT does not.

E/M · 99285

Emergency visit, high decision making. Level follows documentation.

Modifier · 25

Separate E/M on the same day as a procedure.

Certification at a glance

CPC, COC and CCS: exam fees and format.

CPC · AAPC

100 questions, 4 hours, 70% to pass.
Member exam $425 (1 attempt) or $499 (2 attempts). Membership $229 a year ($164 students).

COC · AAPC

100 questions, 4 hours, 70% to pass.
Member exam $425 (1 attempt) or $499 (2 attempts). Membership $229 a year ($164 students).

CCS · AHIMA

107 questions, 4 hours, scaled score 300 of 400.
$299 per attempt for members, $399 for non-members. Membership optional.

Fees are set by AAPC and AHIMA and can change. Confirm on their official sites before you register.
