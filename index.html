    Who can join?

    Freshers, graduates from any background, including non-life-science graduates, and working professionals. We begin from the basics and also offer a cross-training track for people already in coding or billing.

    Do I need a medical background?

    No. Medical terminology and anatomy are taught as part of the programme, step by step.

    Online or classroom?

    Both are available. Batches run online or in the classroom, with trainer-led sessions and real chart practice in either mode. Ask us about the current schedule.

    Are you the certifying body?

    No. Certification exams are set and awarded by AAPC and AHIMA. We prepare you for them with focused review, mock tests and one-to-one support.

    Will you help me find a job?

    We support your career, not just your exam, with resume guidance, interview preparation and access to our hiring pipeline. Results depend on your effort and on employer openings.

    What about fees and refunds?

    Fees and refunds are described in our Terms & conditions. Send an enquiry and we will share the current details.
